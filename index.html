MABALINGWE RULES

Don't get us evicted.

The lodge rules allow fines up to R7,000 and/or eviction. These are the ones most likely to collide with our plans.

NO. 01

Leave the spotlights at home.

No high-power spotlights for our self-drive or herping mission. Ordinary headlamps and torches are for lodge/camp use, not scanning game.

NO SPOTLIGHTS
40 / 20

Drive slowly

40 km/h in the reserve, 20 km/h in camps. Stay on roads, obey closures and give animals right of way.

QUIET

No loud music

The reserve says no loud music ever. Tebuah also prohibits disturbances after 22:00. Keep it to our ears.

🔥

Fire discipline

Braai and boma only. Supervise, avoid windy conditions and douse completely before bed. Never collect veld wood.

🚫

No drones or weapons

No drones, fireworks, laser pointers, firearms, air rifles, bows or slingshots inside the reserve.

🐒

Wildlife stays wild

Do not feed or disturb animals. Close doors and windows against baboons and monkeys when the lodge is empty.

🌿

Herping + walking

Use marked or permitted areas only, replace cover exactly, and do not use hiking trails after 16:00. Walking is at own risk.

🗑

Leave no evidence

No litter, bottles, caps or cigarette butts in the veld. Tebuah charges R500 for litter clean-up.
